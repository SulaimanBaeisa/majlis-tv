# Photo credits (home wallpaper and screensaver)

The home wallpaper and screensaver use these Saudi photographs when no TMDB key is set. The asset library contains
39 photos: the original 9 below, unchanged, plus 30 additional city and landmark photographs. Each photograph retains
its individual Wikimedia Commons license and attribution; the app license does not replace the photo license.

The original 9 are 1920 px wide. The 30 new photos have retained source originals and separate true-4K masters
(3840×2160); the app ships high-quality 1920×1080 sRGB JPEG derivatives for its existing 1080p UI plane. New images
were cropped to 16:9, downsampled (never upscaled), color-converted when an ICC profile was present, and JPEG-encoded
at quality 95 with 4:4:4 chroma. GPS EXIF is not included in the derivatives. Source/license attribution is also
embedded in the new master and app JPEGs as XMP.

Full runtime list: `app/assets/saudi/credits.tsv`. Source hashes, original dimensions, crop coordinates, changes and
per-file license URLs: `docs/wallpaper-manifest-30.json`. These are asset additions only: no UI code or TV deployment.

### Attribution limitation

For `s26.jpg`, the Commons source does not name the photographer. Its source credit names the Saudi Photos Hub
by the Saudi Press Agency; the asset therefore credits that source and explicitly says the photographer is unnamed.
No photographer identity has been invented. The captured source metadata is retained in the provenance manifest.
